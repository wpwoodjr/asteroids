HOW TO PLAY

KEYBOARD CONTROLS

  • A / ← : Rotate Left
  • D / → : Rotate Right
  • W / ↑ : Thrust Forward
  • SPACE : Fire Cannon
  • S / ↓ / SHIFT : Hyperspace
  • P / ESC : Pause / Resume
  • M : Mute Audio
  • C : Color Theme
  • G : Gravity Mode
  • F : Fullscreen

PRO SURVIVAL TIPS

• Authentic Zero-G Inertia: Space has zero friction! Your ship coasts indefinitely until you rotate 180° and apply counter-thrust to brake.
• Gravity Mode [G]: cycles through OFF, LOW, MED, and HIGH gravity wells. Slingshot around the planet and crush hazards into its surface!
• Hyperspace is a risky escape: you teleport randomly across space.
• Small saucers are deadly accurate: destroy them before they line up a shot!
• The arcade heartbeat pulses faster as fewer asteroids remain.
