HOW TO PLAY

KEYBOARD CONTROLS

  • A / ← : Rotate Left
  • D / → : Rotate Right
  • W / ↑ : Thrust Forward
  • SPACE : Fire Cannon
  • S / ↓ / SHIFT : Hyperspace
  • P / ESC : Pause / Resume
  • M : Mute Audio
  • C : Color Palette
  • G : Gravity Mode
  • F : Fullscreen

PRO SURVIVAL TIPS

• Arcade Inertia: Your ship keeps drifting after you let go of thrust and only slowly coasts to a stop (no friction in Gravity Mode). To brake fast, rotate 180° and counter-thrust.
• Gravity Mode [G]: cycles through OFF, LOW, MED, and HIGH gravity wells. Slingshot around the planet, and shoot asteroids before they fall in: each one it swallows costs you its points!
• Hyperspace is a gamble: you vanish for a moment and reappear at random, and the jump can destroy your ship. It's riskier when few asteroids remain.
• Small saucers are deadly accurate: destroy them before they line up a shot!
• Watch your own shots: they wrap around the screen and can hit your ship. Saucer shots smash asteroids too.
• The arcade heartbeat speeds up the longer a wave goes on: don't dawdle!
